# C2 research status — 1 November 2026

This is a dated public progress report. **We do not claim that involutions have been excluded, or that the Conway 99-graph problem has been solved.**

Our target is a simple graph with adjacency matrix A satisfying A² + A = 12I + 2J and degree 14, together with an automorphism sigma of order two. The [model contract](MODEL_CONTRACT.md) states the fixed-point input and the exact quotient/sign equations. No additional transitivity, Cayley or A0 hypothesis is imposed.

## Completed components and remaining work

T16, T18 and T20 refer to the proposed exclusion of an integer-valued, sigma-odd eigenfunction phi with A phi = -4 phi and squared norm sum(phi(v)²) equal to 16, 18 or 20, respectively. They are not names for the matching matrix T in our quotient model.

The nine T20 support representatives are supplied in [support_classes.jsonl](../../results/c2/2026-11-01/support_classes.jsonl), with [support_inputs.json](../../results/c2/2026-11-01/support_inputs.json). The row masks encode B_ij = 1 iff bit j of B[i] is set, for 0 <= i,j < 10. A type a/b means input case a and class b. Positive support edges and the fixed point's positive support neighbours I are recorded in the input file. The negative support is its sigma-image. These are support matrices, not full graph witnesses. The full classification proof and enumeration archives are not included in this status release.

The three audit/receipt files are historical records copied from the project evidence. In particular, the morning T20 receipt says the other eight searches had not started; the later snapshot records their subsequent queue start. Historical records are not rewritten to resemble live state.

**These receipts report locally checked results; they are not substitutes for the CNFs, full proofs, generators and mathematical covering arguments.** The large C2 proof files and full upstream packages are retained in the project archives and are not downloadable from this public snapshot. This release therefore does not yet permit a complete independent public replay of the T16/T18/T20 certificate chain. The T20 type 1/0 binary LRAT alone is 13,871,075,065 bytes. No inaccessible private repository link is offered as public evidence.

Our semantic checkers are separately implemented from the generators. Cake checks the propositional LRAT step; this does not mean the entire mathematical graph-to-CNF reduction has been formalized in a proof assistant. This update was prepared from existing closure records without restarting large proof checks or changing running jobs.

## Slice constraints and external work

The [slice review](SLICE_REVIEW_2026-11-01.md) distinguishes independently checked necessary conditions from external exclusion claims that we have not adopted. Our confirmed same-cell matching filter gives maximum degree two on the seven anchor indices, hence at most seven same-cell pairs. In the tested early states it removed no additional case. The immediate T20-start check also removed none; seven support types did not yet have complete anchor labels, so this was not a test of every complete T20 matching.

New component-search implementations and cross-slice propagation methods may be useful. Their correctness, simultaneous symmetry normalization and performance on our models require separate checks before integration.

The existing [order-three congruence note](../breadth1/O3_tau_mod3_theorem.md), dated 6 September, already establishes tau in {6,27} in the fixed-point-free order-three setting. We make no priority claim for this elementary reduction.

Next milestones are the certified disposition of the eight open T20 types, the remaining mathematical transfer audit, and a complete publicly replayable C2 evidence package. No completion date is asserted.
