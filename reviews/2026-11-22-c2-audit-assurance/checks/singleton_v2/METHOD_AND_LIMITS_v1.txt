Independent singleton-root search: method and assurance scope
22 November 2026

Original mathematical source and priority
Yongxian Zhang (mxym), frozen revision d73a176f942b1fd67bac3b60c07d181487d84b66.
The specifications used are SingletonSearch.md and RootsBridge.md. No original
search header is imported or linked by singleton_exact_v1.cpp. The local
attachments and chart matrices are generated by the referee's own_local.py
and own_setup.py, copied without modification. This is a second search
implementation relative to Zhang, not an independent rederivation of every
input routine or a third independently authored mathematical proof.

Coverage
The separate preliminary unary enumeration covers all 3580 catalogue lines,
all 924 balanced root sign vectors before filtering, and both overall signs.
It finds 272 nonphysical bases and 177842 surviving raw signs on 3308 physical
charts. The new setup enumerates all 720*64 signed permutations of six axes,
retains precisely those commuting with both matching permutations, and
partitions the surviving roots under this stabilizer and overall negation.
For each representative it stores the entire orbit, checks containment,
disjointness and exact coverage. The orbit action does not add a sign to the
unsigned root row. All possible outside signed attachments remain available;
there is no Hamming-lattice word restriction and no assumed global mod-7 rank.

Four heavy labels
Unsigned attachments are grouped only by their literal label and 12 entries
of T; every signed lift is retained. All distinct pairs of positive and
negative heavy labels are matched using exact 18-entry integer tuples.
There is no positional encoding or possible encoding collision.
The matching equation is the root eigenvector equation on the anchors and
Bx=0 on the six peripheral axes. The own-sign heavy load bound and exact
projected root Cauchy inequality are imposed. All three free T entries in
{0,1,2} are tried; the remaining three follow from the four heavy equations.
Every row permits at most one double. The exact T-squared inequalities are
then tested on all 16 selected labels.

Physical and rational tests
The search reconstructs the 39,41,43,45,47,49 and 51-vertex partial graphs.
It checks common-neighbour capacities literally and the necessary degree
bound 14. T=1 allows both signed orientations; T=0 or 2 forces L=0.
The two Gram matrices are projected off their actual anchor spans.
All arithmetic after this projection uses GMP arbitrary-precision rationals.
A symmetric matrix is PSD exactly when successive positive diagonal pivots
have PSD Schur complements; a zero remaining diagonal requires the entire
remaining block to vanish. The selected principal basis is retained, so
range conditions and later projections refer to one common span.

Both ambient rational spaces have dimension 15 and square determinant.
Thus real rank is at most 15. For a full-dimensional heavy quartet, the
unsigned determinant divided by 112^2 and the signed determinant divided by
224^2 must be integer squares, as derived in the source specification.
The signed principal matrix over F7 must have rank at most 13; ordinary
elimination checks that directly, without assuming rank 13 globally.

One or two additional labels
For every outside label, every star attachment and every compatible heavy
T/L assignment is tested. The root eigenvector equation with x=0, T-squared
caps, literal physical caps, and signed mod-7 rank remain necessary.
In each sector the candidate must satisfy the exact range equations and
have a nonnegative residual norm. If the span already has dimension 15,
that norm is zero. At dimension 14, adjoining the vector gives an integer
square determinant. These exact real tests are stronger than the original
comparison-prime-only tests, and are necessary for a genuine graph.
No prime-based test is needed in this implementation.

If every outside label has a candidate for one fixed quartet, the code
searches a clique on the eight smallest domains. A pair is accepted only if
some common T/L choice passes both residual 2-by-2 Gram tests, the original
signed rank bound and the literal 51-vertex graph capacities. Pair link
choices need not be globally consistent; that relaxation can only add
false positives. The clique search has no depth or time cut-off.

Controls and observed pilots
- 2540 exact controls: 1258 admissible and 1282 inadmissible. Oracle:
  all principal minors computed with Python Fraction arithmetic, plus
  genuine ambient Gram constructions, rank/degeneracy and square tests.
- All 58 frozen local positive fixtures pass physical capacities, both
  Gram sectors, determinant divisibility and signed rank.
- Chart 0 closes: 19440 matches, 58320 T quartets, 12240 T-cap survivors.
- Chart 183 closes: 329640 matches, 1119792 quartets, 88208 T-cap survivors.
- Chart 3377 closes: 243786 matches, 886968 quartets, 87832 T-cap survivors.
These three integer triples agree with the original records exactly.
Charts 183 and 3377 needed a second layer in the original implementation.
Here they close under the stronger exact first-layer extension conditions;
the second-layer clique branch was therefore not exercised by these pilots.
Do not describe this as reproducing the original second-layer survivor
ledger. It gives independent closure of those cases by stronger necessary
conditions. The complete catalogue run and final coverage audit are pending.

Arithmetic bound in the borrowed NumPy setup
F_A+J is positive definite with diagonal 11; H_A+J_comp is positive definite
with diagonal 6. By Hadamard, their inverse numerators after common
denominator reduction have magnitude at most 11^11 and 6^11 respectively:
the adjugate is PSD, so off-diagonal cofactors are bounded by its diagonal
cofactors. Anchor cross entries have absolute value at most 5.
The complete quadratic form accumulation is bounded by
144*25*11^11 < 2^63 (and the unsigned bound is smaller). Thus int64 in the
borrowed setup cannot overflow on these forms. The new C++ search itself
uses arbitrary-precision rationals for these calculations, active assertions,
undefined-behaviour checking and overflow traps for ordinary integers.

Execution
The first three pilots use run_one_singleton_v1.py. The complete queue uses
the separately versioned v2 runner, which adds gzip only after successful
completion and verifies the complete decompressed byte stream and SHA-256
before removing the uncompressed input. The controller binds source hashes,
refuses pre-existing unclaimed jobs, records process birth identity, measures
multiple CPU intervals, and retains at least the documented CPU/RAM/disk
reserves. Error, missing output, OPEN or resource refusal is not exclusion.
Full closure is not claimed until all 3308 search results and the 272
nonphysical cases have been independently reconciled, with all hashes.
